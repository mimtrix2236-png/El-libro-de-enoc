# Ambient celestial audio feature

## Purpose
Optional, instrumental-only ambient sound for reading. The mood should be calm, contemplative, and celestial, appropriate to the Book of Enoch—not dramatic or distracting.

## UI / behavior
- A compact sound toggle in the reader toolbar or settings: “Ambiente celestial”.
- Default OFF. Clearly indicate enabled state.
- Playback is instrumental ambience only: no speech, lyrics, narration, or abrupt transitions.
- Volume control, with a gentle fade-in/fade-out; looping should have a seamless transition.
- Stop/pause when the user disables it, exits the reader, or when audio focus is lost; avoid unexpectedly playing in the background.
- Keep the reading experience usable offline and minimize battery use. Prefer a small, compressed, seamless loop.

## Rights and source policy
Use an original generated audio asset or an asset with an explicit license permitting redistribution and app inclusion. Record the creator/source, license, attribution requirements, and download date in `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Do not label an audio file “copyright-free” merely because it is downloadable; verify the license. Avoid Content ID / platform restrictions where possible and retain license evidence.

## Sound design brief
A quiet, ethereal pad with soft airy harmonics, faint bell-like shimmer, slow evolving tones, subtle cosmic ambience, no recognizable melody or religious quotations, no percussion, no voices. Low volume and smooth loop. This is a design brief; an audio file must still be created/obtained, licensed, integrated, and tested before this feature can be claimed complete.
